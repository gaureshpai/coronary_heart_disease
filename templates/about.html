About This Project

Coronary Heart Disease Prediction System

This application uses machine learning to predict the 10-year risk of coronary heart disease (CHD) based on patient health data from the Framingham Heart Study.

How It Works

The prediction model analyzes multiple risk factors including:

  • Demographics: Age, gender
  • Smoking status: Current smoking, cigarettes per day
  • Medical history: Blood pressure medication, stroke history, hypertension, diabetes
  • Vital signs: Blood pressure (systolic/diastolic), heart rate
  • Lab values: Total cholesterol, glucose levels
  • Body measurements: BMI (Body Mass Index)

The Model

Our prediction model is trained using the Framingham Heart Study dataset, which is one of the most extensively studied cardiovascular disease studies in the world. The model uses logistic regression to estimate the probability of developing CHD within 10 years.
